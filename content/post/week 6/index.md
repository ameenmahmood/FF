### Week 6 Preview and Trades
Coming off a couple of key wins, several teams sought to continue their hot streak by beefing up their rosters after some key injuries. A blockbuster trade from Neb's Saquon for Danny's Derrick Henry and Jordan Addison, moving one of last year's runner-up for MVP. Will this be enough to snap Danny's 5 loss win streak? Only time will tell. Otherwise, it was a quiet week of trading. 

**Levi vs Lonny**

Levi had an exciting night of TNF, with one of the most electric RB to watch recently with Skattebo and Robinson paying off big time. Thursday night set the tone for the rest of the week for Levi, as he went into Sunday and Monday with a comfortable lead. Unfortunately, disaster struck Lonny with Puka going down early and remains questionable. We could be witnessing the WR1 of the league go down, and with a 1-4 start, there could be rough times ahead for Lonny. Levi easily secured the W, snapping his lost streak. 

**Jed vs Danny**

Poor Danny, with his 0-5 start, we are all rooting for Danny, but with a powerhouse matchup, there was not much hope for Danny to escape his rut. Jed did lucky, with Drake Maye posting a season high in fantasy points, and a standout performance from Pickens. However, Danny made the mistake of benching Goedert and Dart, and while starting them would not have been enough to secure the win, it shows there could be brighter times ahead of Danny.

**Jonah vs Ben Brown iCloud**

Ben Brown iCloud is coming off a HOT 4 game win streak, and secures another W. From season highs from Josh Jacobs and JSN, and even starting Kyler was not enough for Jonah to overcome. Ben Brown iCloud continues the hot streak with a record of 1-5. Jonah, who was the last undefeated team in the league, has seen his 2nd lost in a row. Can he right the ship in time to secure his place as one of the top teams, or are the supports already giving way? 


**Jack vs Nolan**

Two players who tend to silently observe, this was a dominant performance from Nolan. With some season-high performances from Achane and Bijan, even Mahomes and Rico season high performances were not enough to bolster the rest of Jack's lackluster performances, especially with JJ out. Both teams don't have much momentum going for them, but this could signal for better time for Nolan as he secures the W and rises to 2-4, with Jack ticking his 2nd L of the season at 4-2. 

**Woods vs Hern**

Ahh, Woods and Hern, some of the biggest trash-talkers with a chip on their shoulder. Hern's late involvement hasn't slowed his roll at all. With a hot 3-game win streak going into week 6, he's proven that he can hang with the OGs of the league. The Senate Woods on the other hand, comes in with a 2-loss streak, and with extremely mediocre performances from key players, he comes up with a disappointing 86 points, taking his record down to 2-4. Hern needed several players to get hot on MNF, and hot they did. Some standout performances from Jayden Daniels and London secured Hern the win, continuing the hot streak and taking his record to a very solid 4-2. 

**Neb vs Ameen**

With the hardest mid off so far this season, this was a measaly 79-70 points, with extremely poor performances on both sides. Ameen came in looking to continue his hot streak from last week, after taking down the last undefeated team without Nabers. Neb came in looking to continue to correct course, and Neb securing the W, taking his record to 3-3, he's looking to be well on track. Ameen taking his 2nd loss of the season and dropping to 4-2, could hint at cracks in the roster. 

We’re just about to hit the midway mark of the regular season. Will we see the Big 2 continue their dominance? Will the mid teams make a sneaky playoff run? Or will the bottom teams turn their season around and aim for the stars? Only time will tell, but in the meantime let’s keep the trash talk flowing and the beer chugs going.
