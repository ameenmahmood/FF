### Week 7 Preview and Trades
No trades went through this week but the chat was buzzing. Everyone is floating offers pretending they are being strategic when really they are just bored and trying to unload whoever scored five points on Sunday. It is that midseason itch where nobody is happy with their team but nobody wants actually to shake it up. Give it another week and someone is going to panic.


### Week 7 Matchups

**Ameen 97 def Lonny 93**
Tight game here. Ameen edges out Lonny by four keeping pace at 5 and 2 while Lonny falls to a grim 1 and 6. It has been that kind of season for him. Close and competitive but always one guy short of pulling it off. Kittle giving him a zero did not help. Ameen is not flashy but he finds ways to win and that is what matters right now.

**Levi 108 def Danny 79**
Another rough one for Danny. 0 and 7 now and it is starting to get sad. He is setting lineups and trying but the football gods just keep kicking him in the shins. He's got the potential but having three 25+ scoring players on the bench is brutal. Levi moves to 3 and 4, quietly clawing back to 500. Not a statement win but he will take it. Danny might need an emotional support animal at this point.

**Ben Brown 115 def Neb 66**
The Ben Bowl was supposed to be competitive but Ben Brown turned it into a massacre. 115 to 66 is just rude. Neb’s team never showed up simple as that. Ben Brown is now 6 and 1 looking like a legit title threat while Neb falls to 3 and 4 and probably hates fantasy again. We have all been there.

**Jed 164 def Nolan 82**
Jed’s team went nuclear. 164 points and you can’t even be mad, you just clap. Nolan didn’t stand a chance even if he had started everyone right. Jed is sitting at 6 and 1 and looks unstoppable right now. Nolan drops to 2 and 5 and just got buried. That one was over before Red Zone even signed off.

**Jonah 125 def Hern 82**
Jonah keeps stacking wins moving to 5 and 2. Solid all around no weak spots this week. Hern is 4 and 3 now still fine record wise but that lineup looked tired. Maybe it was just one of those weeks where every player forgets how to football. Jonah’s quietly building something that might be scary in a few weeks.

**Jack 110 def Woods 89**
Jack gets it done again 110 to 89. Nothing crazy just steady production and another win in the books. Woods falls to 2 and 5 and can’t seem to find his footing. Maybe too many distractions from whatever Deep State operations he is running but at some point he has to figure it out. Jack is flying under the radar and that might be the best place to be right now.

Week 7 gave us a little separation with Jed and Ben Brown cruising and Ameen and Jonah right behind. Everyone else is fighting for their lives. The middle of the standings is a warzone and a few of these teams are one more dud week away from hitting the panic button.


### Primetime Matchup of Week 8
This is it the matchup of misery. 0 and 7 Danny vs 1 and 6 Lonny. Somebody is finally walking out of here with a win or maybe they will both somehow lose who knows. Lonny is without Puka this week which could even things out. For Danny this might be his last real shot before the season turns into a full on comedy special.
